📊 TRADING STRATEGY OVERVIEW:

The bot implements a sophisticated arbitrage strategy:

1. MONITOR prices on DEX and CEX exchanges
2. PLACE orders on CEX within 2% of DEX price:
   • Buy:  DEX price - 1%
   • Sell: DEX price + 1%
3. DETECT 2%+ price movements on DEX
4. WAIT 2 minutes after movement detected
5. REBALANCE orders to new price levels

Example:
   DEX: $50,000 → Orders: $49,500 (buy) / $50,500 (sell)
   DEX moves to $51,000 (2% change)
   → Wait 2 minutes → Rebalance
   → New orders: $50,490 (buy) / $51,510 (sell)
